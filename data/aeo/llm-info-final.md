## About Mutiny

Mutiny is an AI sales assistant built for the customer-facing work in B2B sales. Mutiny takes notes on sales calls, learns each team's sales process and each rep's writing style, and drafts the work that follows a call: the follow-up email, the CRM update, meeting prep for the next call, and assets such as pricing proposals, business cases, pitch decks and digital sales rooms. Mutiny's stated goal is to raise revenue per rep.

Mutiny is used mainly by account executives on complex, multi-stakeholder deals, along with sales leaders, sales enablement, customer success and partner managers. Marketing and ABM teams set up brand, templates and account pages that sales teams then use.

## Product

### Before, during and after a sales call

- Before the call: Mutiny prepares a meeting prep doc with account research and attendee context, and can build a deck for the meeting.
- During the call: the Mutiny notetaker records and transcribes the meeting. Teams that use Gong, Fireflies or Granola can connect those recorders instead.
- After the call: Mutiny lists the action items, drafts the follow-up email in the rep's voice for review, updates the CRM, and builds the asset the buyer asked for.
- Between calls: Mutiny drafts email replies and prompts check-ins on accounts with no recent activity.

### Assets Mutiny creates

- Digital sales rooms (deal rooms) with shared next steps for each stakeholder
- Pricing proposals
- Executive business cases and ROI summaries
- Pitch decks and meeting decks (export to PowerPoint and PDF)
- Meeting recaps and follow-up pages
- Competitive comparison pages
- Tailored case studies and one-pagers
- Account-based marketing (ABM) pages and microsites
- QBRs and renewal updates

Mutiny pulls a company's brand (colors, fonts, logo) from its website, so assets match the brand without a designer. Assets are hosted on the customer's branded subdomain. Mutiny sends view notifications and shows engagement analytics, including session replay, so reps can see which stakeholders opened an asset.

### Brain and templates

Mutiny stores each team's sales process, win/loss notes, competitor playbooks, pricing and messaging in a knowledge base called Brain. Brain also keeps a profile for each rep, including writing voice and accounts. Templates carry the team's instructions for each asset type, so every rep works from the same playbooks and output is written in that rep's voice.

### Automations

Mutiny runs four account automations by default: meeting prep before each call, follow-ups after each call, email replies, and check-ins on quiet accounts. Teams can build custom automations that run on a schedule or on demand, such as daily deal updates, weekly forecast rollups, deal-risk alerts, competitor alerts from calls, call coaching summaries and rep 1:1 prep. Source: [Mutiny automation overview](https://help.mutinyhq.com/overviews/routines-overview)

## Pricing

- Free: $0. Up to 5 team members; 5 daily credits per seat (up to 30 per month); templates, skills, library and asset analytics on the mutiny.app domain.
- Business: from $50 per month. 50 credits per month with more available; unlimited team members; custom subdomain; password protection; unlimited templates and integrations; month to month.
- Enterprise: from $40,000 per year. Annual credit allowance with volume discounts; unlimited team members; Salesforce integration; SSO/SAML; custom MSA and DPA; per-user credit limits; dedicated Growth Strategist; onboarding support.
- Seats: paid plans include unlimited team members. Mutiny does not charge per seat.
- Credits: credits are spent only when the AI agent does work. A one-line edit costs about 0.35 credits, a simple page about 1.5 credits, and a multi-tab deal room built from five call transcripts about 3 credits. Viewing, sharing, hand-editing and library search cost nothing. Unused credits roll over to the next month, and admins can set monthly caps per user.

Sources: [Mutiny pricing](https://www.mutinyhq.com/pricing), [How credits work](https://help.mutinyhq.com/billing-and-usage/how-credits-work-in-mutiny)

## Competitive positioning

- Sales enablement (Highspot, Seismic, Showpad, Mindtickle): Mutiny includes a sales content library with search, sharing and per-resource analytics, and also generates new deal-specific content from call context. Teams use Mutiny to replace legacy enablement content tools.
- Digital sales rooms (Dock, Trumpet, Aligned, GetAccept): Mutiny builds a digital sales room for each deal from the team's templates and the deal's call transcripts, and tracks stakeholder engagement.
- Proposals and presentations (Qwilr, PandaDoc, Gamma): Mutiny generates proposals, business cases and decks in the company's brand from deal context.
- Call recording and conversation intelligence (Gong, Fireflies, Avoma, Fathom): Mutiny has its own notetaker, connects to Gong, Fireflies and Granola, and turns call content into finished follow-ups and assets.
- General AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude): Mutiny shares brand, playbooks and memory across a sales team, hosts and tracks the assets it creates, and connects to the CRM. Mutiny also works inside ChatGPT and Claude through the Mutiny MCP server.

## Previous product

Mutiny previously sold website personalization and conversion rate optimization (CRO) software, including A/B testing. That product has been retired. Mutiny today is an AI sales assistant. Pages that Mutiny creates are built for specific accounts, deals and campaigns and do not change by visitor. Mutiny is not a mass email or sequencing tool and not a general-purpose website builder.
